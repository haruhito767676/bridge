A warning appears during installation or launch

Bridge is a free app made by an individual, and it has not been signed or reviewed by Apple or Microsoft. That is why you may see the warnings below. They do not mean there is a problem with the app itself.

macOS: “Apple could not verify it is free of malware that may harm your Mac”

  1. Double-click Bridge, and close the warning when it appears
  2. Open “System Settings” → “Privacy & Security” and scroll down
  3. Next to “Bridge was blocked…”, choose “Open Anyway”
  4. Confirm with Touch ID or your password, and it opens

Once you have opened it this way, you can launch it by double-clicking as usual from then on. On macOS 14 or earlier, right-clicking (or Control-clicking) Bridge in Finder and choosing “Open” works too.

If you are comfortable with Terminal, running xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Bridge.app has the same effect.

Windows: “Windows protected your PC”

  1. Click “More info” in the dialog
  2. Click the “Run anyway” button that appears

Other devices can’t be found

First, check the following.

  • Both devices are connected to the same Wi-Fi / LAN
  • You have added the device (pairing) on both, or the “Sync key” (同期キー) on the “Advanced settings” (詳細設定) page is exactly the same on both devices
  • You are not on a guest Wi-Fi network (these often isolate devices from each other, so they can’t be discovered automatically)

If they still can’t be found, press the “Scan now” (いま探す) button in the settings sheet. It scans the network once.

If that doesn’t help, the network may be blocking UDP multicast. In that case, open “Advanced settings” (詳細設定) in the settings sheet and enter the other device’s IP address under “Add manually” (手動で追加) — you can connect that way too.

Can’t add a device

  • Start on the device you already use by pressing “Add” (追加), and on the new device by pressing “Find” (探す). “Add” stays open for 2 minutes. If the time runs out, press “Add” again
  • If the other device does not show up in the list, check that both are on the same Wi-Fi / LAN and not on a guest network. If it still can’t be found, copy the “Sync key” (同期キー) on the “Advanced settings” (詳細設定) page and paste it on the other device
  • If the two codes differ, press “Mismatch” (不一致). No key is passed. Someone on the network may be trying to get in between, so try again on a network you trust
  • After 3 mismatches in a row, “Add” stops for safety. Press “Add” again to restart
  • If it says the other device is busy (相手は、接続中です), another device is in the middle of being added. Try again in a while

When a device joins with “Find”, its sync key is replaced with the key of the device that pressed “Add”. If it was syncing with other devices before, it will no longer connect to them.

Syncing is slow, or nothing shows up

  • Check that your OS firewall is not blocking Bridge’s traffic (if a permission dialog appeared the first time you launched it, choose “Allow”)
  • In the settings sheet, open “Advanced settings” (詳細設定) and check that the other device appears under “Connected devices” (接続中のデバイス). If it isn’t connected, the last reason for the failure is shown
  • If it says “The other device is on an old version” (相手のバージョンが古いです), update the other device to version 2.0 or later too. 1.x and 2.0 use different communication methods, so they cannot sync with each other
  • If it says “The clocks on the two devices differ” (2 台の時計がずれています), set the date and time on both to automatic (if they differ by a minute or more, they will not connect, for safety). If it says “The other device is old, or the sync key differs” (相手が古いか、同期キーが違います), check the two points above and that the sync keys are the same

To dig into the cause, open bridge.log from “Show log” (ログを表示) under “Advanced settings” (詳細設定) in the settings sheet. It records what is happening — discovery, syncing, reasons for failure — in chronological order.

I want to uninstall

macOS

  1. Right-click the Bridge icon in the menu bar and choose “Quit Bridge”
  2. Drag Bridge from the “Applications” folder to the Trash

To remove your settings and history as well, also delete the ~/Library/Application Support/bridge/ folder.

Windows

  1. Open “Settings” → “Apps”, choose Bridge from the list, and select “Uninstall”

To remove your settings and history as well, also delete the %APPDATA%\bridge\ folder.

Still not solved

If none of the above solves it, please contact us with a GitHub issue. If you can, include the contents of bridge.log (see above) and your OS and version — it makes the investigation much smoother.
